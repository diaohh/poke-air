## Status (2026-09-28): implemented, pending manual validation

Both parts (A — battle information, B — Phase 2 team builder) are implemented. As agreed with the team,
**no new unit tests were written yet**: they come after the manual validation (see § Tests to add). The

Verified while building it: `pnpm check` green (76 tests; `model.test.ts` adapted to the new effect
shape), the existing `pnpm test:e2e` passes, and a throwaway Playwright run (not committed) went through
the editor (search, item, nature, 4 moves, SP, save), the Species Clause hint, export, saved teams, an
invalid import (Z-Crystal) and a valid one, and a battle showing `Sandstorm · 2–5`, `Reflect · 1–4`,
`Spikes ×2`, `Attack +4` on the Host and stats / nature / `▲ Atk +4` on the phone. Generated data:
1234 species, 827 moves, 363 items, ~834 KB (**~157 KB gzip**), ≈ 9 s.

Details that differ from the first draft of this plan:

- The move pool must union the Champions movepool with `Dex.mod('gen9')`'s National Dex movepool: the
  Champions mod replaces its roster's learnsets (Garchomp had 58 candidate moves instead of 94).
- Showdown rejects 0 SP + default nature ("did you forget…"): `validateSet` uses Hardy (neutral) then.
- The editor's Species Clause hint only knows the player's own slots (teammates' teams are private); the
  server checks the whole side.
- Species without a downloaded sprite show a letter badge until `pnpm fetch:sprites` is re-run.

## General analysis

What exists after Phase 1 and what each part touches:

| Area                 | Phase 1 state                                                | Change                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Own battle request   | Exact HP, item, ability, moves (enriched with dex move data) | + computed stats, nature (±stat) from the owner's set, current stat stages of the active Pokémon (from the sim instance)                     |
| Host spectator model | Weather / terrain / field / side conditions as plain names   | + start turn per effect, hazard layers, turns left from dex durations sent with the log (`battle:log.effects`)                               |
| Team contents        | Randomizer sets only (trusted), `team:setSlot` only removes  | Any legal set: `team:setSlot` with a full set, `team:import` (Showdown text), server-side `TeamValidator` + Species Clause across the side   |
| Team builder data    | None on the phone (server enriches battle requests)          | Static compact dex JSON generated by `packages/data` (`pnpm build:data`), loaded by the phone in TEAM_BUILDING                               |
| Phone team builder   | Cards with reroll/remove, randomize, ready                   | Cards with edit/remove, per-Pokémon editor (species, item, ability, nature, 4 moves, Stat Points), import/export, saved teams (localStorage) |
| Sprites              | Randomizer species + their Megas / battle formes             | Every legal species + formes (`battleRoster()` grows; `pnpm fetch:sprites` downloads the new ones incrementally)                             |

Cross-cutting rules kept: server authoritative (validation always on the server), privacy (natures and
sets never reach the Host), no hardcoded Pokémon data (durations, legality, learnsets, natures all come
from the Showdown dex), every string an i18n key.

## Spike S3 results (roster & validation, 2026-09-28)

Measured with scripts against `pokemon-showdown` 0.11.11 (details also in `docs/05`):

- **Validator format**: `gen9championsnatdexdraft@@@!Nickname Clause, !OHKO Clause, !Evasion Clause, !Sleep Clause Mod, Z-Move Clause`,
  used **per set** (`validateSet`). NatDex Draft's own `checkCanLearn` falls back to the National Dex learnsets,
  so roster species keep their full movepool and non-roster species validate too. 0 / 360 randomizer
  sets rejected.
- Plain `gen9championscustomgame@@@Obtainable, NatDex Mod…` also validates, but **roster species only get
  their Champions learnset** (e.g. Garchomp can't learn Aqua Tail) → not used.
- **Z-Crystals are legal** under `+Past` → `Z-Move Clause` bans them. Mega Stones, Plates, Drives, Orbs,
  Rusted Sword, legendaries, event formes (Floette-Eternal at Lv 50) pass. CAP, LGPE-only formes and
  "does not exist in the National Dex" species are rejected.
- `Min Team Size = 1` **cannot override** Standard AG's `Min Team Size = 6` in that format →
  `validateTeam` is not used; team size and Species Clause (across teammates) are checked by `Room`.
- SP limits come with the mod ("more than 32 Stat Points in HP", "… total … limit of 66").
- Legal roster: **1234 species/formes** (1253 candidates after dropping battle-only, Mega, Primal, Gmax,
  CAP/LGPE/Pokéstar/Custom; 19 more rejected by NatDex existence). Learnsets: ~72 legal moves per species,
  818 distinct moves, **~77 KB gzip** as move indexes; generation ≈ 8 s.
- **Champions stat formula** (`mods/champions/scripts.ts`, no Level Clause): `HP = base + SP + 75`,
  other stats `= floor((base + SP + 20) × nature)` (×1.1 / ×0.9).
- **Effect durations** come from the dex: `condition.duration` (weather/terrain/screens/Trick Room/Gravity
  5, Tailwind 4, Safeguard 5) and `durationCallback` gives the item-extended value (Light Clay, weather
  rocks, Terrain Extender → 8). Hazards have no duration (layers only).

## Part A — Battle information iteration

### A1. Stats, nature and stat stages on the phone

- `shared/battle.ts`: `BattlePokemon` gains `stats: StatTable` (HP = max HP), `nature?: NatureInfo`
  (`{ name, plus?, minus? }`) and `boosts?` (active Pokémon only).
- `core/battle/request.ts`: `enrichRequest(raw, rqid, extras)` reads `side.pokemon[].stats` from the raw
  request, looks the nature up in the owner's own set (`MatchController` keeps `side → name → nature`
  from `room.battleSides()`), and the current boosts from the sim (`BattleSession.activeBoosts(side)`).
  Owner-only data: it only travels in `battle:request`.
- Phone `PokemonSheet` (switch menu and forced switch): six stat boxes; the stat raised by the nature
  shows **▲ + green**, the lowered one **▼ + red** (icon + color), nature name below; the active
  Pokémon also shows its stat stages (`Atk +2`).

### A2. Field effects with turns left on the Host

- `shared/battle.ts`: `EffectDuration { min, max }`; `BattleLogPayload.effects` maps effect names started
  in those lines to their duration (like `moves`). Computed in `core` from the dex (see spike).
- `HostBattleModel`: weather / terrain / field effects / side conditions become `FieldEffect
{ name, since, layers }`. `since` is the first turn the effect counts for (effects started after the
  turn's residuals — `|upkeep|` — or before turn 1 count from the next turn); repeated `-sidestart` adds
  hazard layers.
- Turns left = `duration − (turn − since)`; unknown item extension shows a range (`3–6`) until the base
  duration is exceeded (then the extended value is certain). No duration → hazards show `×layers`.
- Host UI: top chips `Rain · 4`, `Trick Room · 2`; side card chips `Reflect · 3–6`, `Spikes ×2`.
- Not done (optional in the roadmap): mirroring the chips on the phones.

## Part B — Phase 2: full team builder

### B1. Rules and validation (`packages/core`)

- `SHOWDOWN_FORMATS.validator` = the Casual validator format above.
- `TeamService.validateSet(set)` → normalized `PokemonSetData` (dex names, level 50, name = species, no
  IVs/shiny) or `RoomError('INVALID_SET', { details })` with Showdown's problem lines.
- `TeamService.importTeam(text)` → sets parsed with Showdown's `Teams.import`, normalized + validated.
- `Room.setSlot(playerId, slot, set | null)` (null = remove) and `Room.importTeam(playerId, sets)`
  (replaces the team, keeps the first `quota` Pokémon, reports how many were skipped). Both enforce
  **Species Clause across the side** (`SPECIES_CLAUSE { species }`) and un-ready the player.
- Randomizer output stays trusted (0 rejections measured).

### B2. Protocol (`packages/shared`, `apps/server`)

| Event                        | Change                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `team:setSlot { slot, set }` | `set` = full `PokemonSetData` (zod-bounded) or `null`                                                 |
| `team:import { text }` (new) | Showdown text (≤ 10 000 chars); ack `{ count, skipped }`; also used to load saved teams               |
| New error codes              | `INVALID_SET` (`params.details`: Showdown's English problem text), `SPECIES_CLAUSE`, `INVALID_IMPORT` |

Deviation from "codes only": `INVALID_SET` carries Showdown's English validator text in `params.details`
(decision D-38). The phone pre-checks the common cases (SP limits, no moves) with translated messages, so
this text only appears on edge cases (event-only move combinations, forme/item requirements).

### B3. Team builder data (`packages/data`, `packages/core`)

- `core/team/dex-data.ts` `buildTeamBuilderData()` → `TeamBuilderData` (`shared/dex.ts`): legal species
  (types, base stats, abilities, learnset as move indexes, Mega Stones), moves (type, category, power,
  accuracy, PP, priority, target, short description), legal held items (no Poké Balls, no Z-Crystals),
  ability descriptions, natures.
- `pnpm build:data` (`packages/data/scripts/build-dex.ts`) writes `apps/web/public/data/teambuilder.json`
  (git-ignored). Root `dev` and `build` run it first; it skips when the file is current (stamp =
  Showdown version + generator version).
- `battleRoster()` = every legal species + their Mega / battle-only formes → `pnpm fetch:sprites` fetches
  the new sprites (incremental; many more files than Phase 1).

### B4. Phone UI (`apps/web/src/controller/team-builder/`)

- **Team list:** cards with ✏️ edit and ✕ remove; empty dashed slot "+ Add Pokémon" opens the editor, its
  🎲 adds a random one; gold Randomize fills the empty slots (or rerolls the full team); a "Team" menu
  (import, export, save, saved teams).
- **Editor** (full view): species, item, ability, nature, 4 moves, Stat Points (66 total / 32 max, stepper
  and slider, live Lv 50 stats with nature ▲▼), 🎲 random set for the slot, Save / Cancel / Remove.
  Pickers are full-screen searchable lists (sprite, types, move type/category/power).
- **Import / export:** paste Showdown text → `team:import`; export copies the current team as text.
- **Saved teams:** named Showdown texts in `localStorage` (max 30); loading one sends `team:import`.
- Data: `lib/team-dex.ts` loads the JSON once (module cache) with indexes; `lib/stats.ts` holds the
  Champions stat formula; `shared/team-text.ts` formats Showdown text.

## Feedback round 1 (2026-09-28, implemented, pending validation)

- **Nature picker by effect** (`team-builder/NaturePicker.tsx`): pick the stat to raise ▲ and the one to
  lower ▼ (5 buttons each), the matching nature appears with its effect on this Pokémon's stats
  ("Atk 182 → 200"), confirm with "Use Adamant". The same stat twice or the "Neutral nature" chip =
  neutral (Hardy, or the current neutral nature: all neutral natures are identical).
- **Battle Pokémon sheet:** one row per stat with a **Stat Points bar** (0–32) and "+N", nature row
  colors (▲ green / ▼ red, nothing for neutral). The owner's request now carries `statPoints` from their
  set (owner-only, like the nature: `RequestExtras.setOf`).
- **More sound feedback on the Host:** stat stages as arpeggios sized by the stage change (±1 / ±2 / ±3+),
  ability call-outs, Protect, cures, items, weather, terrain / rooms, screens vs hazards (told apart by
  whether the effect has a dex duration), effect endings. The Host model now also narrates abilities
  announced through `-activate` and items (`-enditem`: eaten / used up / lost; `-item`: revealed /
  obtained), which also shows them in the battle log.
- **Compact team list:** `Room` keeps each player's Pokémon first and the empty slots last after every
  change (remove, save, random): removing the middle one of three moves the third up, and a Pokémon
  added in any empty slot lands right after the others.
- **Mega Rayquaza** (D-41): it Mega Evolves knowing Dragon Ascent, without a Mega Stone. Battle format
  ids now add `+Past` (the Champions `canMegaEvo` requires it for Megas by move; verified in a real
  battle, also that it can't without the move or while holding a Z-Crystal). The builder data carries
  `megaMove` and the editor explains it ("Mega Evolves knowing Dragon Ascent: no Mega Stone needed").
- Checked with a throwaway Playwright run: Adamant picked by effect, stat rows with bars in battle,
  Intimidate narrated ("[Gyarados's Intimidate]" → "Garchomp's Attack fell!").

## Manual validation checklist

1. `pnpm build:data` (or just `pnpm dev`) creates `apps/web/public/data/teambuilder.json`;
   `pnpm fetch:sprites` downloads the new sprites.
2. Team builder: add a Pokémon by search, pick item/ability/nature/moves/SP, save; edit it again; remove.
3. SP: can't exceed 32 per stat / 66 total; stats update with the nature (▲ green / ▼ red).
4. Server rejections: e.g. two Garchomp on one side (Species Clause, also across teammates in doubles).
5. Export → copy text; Import the same text → same team; save a team, reload it later, delete it.
6. Battle: the Pokémon sheet shows stats with nature colors and, for the active one, stat stages.
7. Host: Rain / Trick Room / Reflect / Tailwind chips count down; Spikes show ×1…×3; Light Clay screens
   show `5–8` → exact after turn 5.
